What Is Included: The Three Systems

Built so you maintain full control without ever touching complicated marketing software.

3. Menu Photo Board
One simple screen on your phone that lists every dish on your menu.
A counter at the top shows how many dishes still need photos. You tap a dish to move it from No Photo to Photo Taken, Edited, and Live.
4. New Dish Photo Check
A 10-second phone form for you or your counter staff whenever a new dish goes on the menu.
Flags any dish missing a photo, logs it to a Google Sheet, and stops blank dishes from sitting unnoticed online. A blank name or zero price shows an immediate error.
5. Weekly Poster Ideas
Every Monday morning, our system checks current food trends and upcoming food days.
Turns them into 5 short poster and reel ideas tailored specifically to your menu, arriving directly in your inbox in plain words.
