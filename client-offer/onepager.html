What Is Included
1. Full Menu Photo Audit & Visit:

We review your current Zomato and Swiggy listings and identify every item lacking a proper photo. We visit during your preferred hours to take quick, natural photos of up to _____ dishes directly from your kitchen without slowing down service. We enhance each photo into a clean, studio-grade food image.

2. Dish Posters & Short Video Ads:

For each photographed dish, you receive: (1) one high-resolution photo formatted for Swiggy and Zomato, (2) one branded food poster for Instagram and WhatsApp status, and (3) one short video ad reel showcasing the dish in motion.

3. Menu Photo Board:

One simple screen on your phone that lists every dish on your menu. A counter at the top shows how many dishes still need photos. You tap a dish to move it from No Photo to Photo Taken, Edited, and Live.

4. New Dish Photo Check:

A 10-second phone form for you or your counter staff whenever a new dish goes on the menu. It records dish name, menu section, price, and photo status. It flags any dish missing a photo, logs it to a Google Sheet, and stops blank dishes from sitting unnoticed online. A blank name or zero price shows an immediate error.

5. Weekly Poster Ideas:

Every Monday morning, our system checks current food trends and upcoming food days, turning them into 5 short poster and reel ideas tailored specifically to your menu, arriving directly in your inbox in plain words.
